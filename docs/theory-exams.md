# Recorded online theory examinations
- 20 server-selected questions per attempt; no adaptive selection or immediate feedback in exam mode. Learning remains unchanged. Historical practice results never imported into the exam archive.
- Authenticated active learners submit only answers. The backend selects and snapshots questions, scores against the private snapshot, and retains a single immutable submission per attempt. Staff see completed attempts and original answer details, and explicitly assess pass/fail. Assessment changes are audited. No automatic certificate or practical qualification.
- Results stored by school and participant via RLS; direct client inserts/updates are denied. Account deletion cascades attempts.
- The existing question bank and answers remain available for learning; this is not a proctored or cheat-proof examination. The school must approve the questions and translations, define its pass standard, examination conditions and record retention. A reload resumes the same question selection but unsaved answers are not persisted. No timer was added.
